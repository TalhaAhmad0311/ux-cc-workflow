# How this product is run

This product is run by **one Head of Product session** that plays PM, discipline managers and Lead, and gets the
hands-on work done through short-lived **sub-agents**. Everything is in `team/ORG-CHART.md`.

- **Told you're the Head of Product?** Follow `team/START.md`. Took over from a predecessor? Read your note in
  `team/handover/` first.
- **The Personal Assistant?** Your rules are in `assistant/CLAUDE.md`.
- **Are you a sub-agent?** Your brief is in `team/work/<NNN>-<job>/brief.md`. Edit only the files it lists, write
  `report.md` next to it, and reply with one line plus that path. Don't start other agents.
- The product's memory lives in files: `team/notes/` (board, background, rules) and `team/work/` (one folder per job).
